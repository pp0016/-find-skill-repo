3. TubeLab Free: estimated RPM for finance/economics niche
4. 1of10: outlier scores on micro channel's top videos
5. Social Blade: growth trajectory of Business Casual and Explains 101

**Counter-Argument (Why This Might Fail):**

## Overall Strategic Recommendation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The tension in your requirements:** You want high RPM + high views + results within 1 month. These three goals partially conflict. High-RPM niches (finance, economics) require deep scripting that slows production. Fast-production niches (explainers, birds) typically have lower RPM. No niche delivers all three at maximum simultaneously.

### If Running ONE Channel (Recommended):

**Pick M1: Animated Economic Documentary / Financial History**

Why:
- Highest verified RPM range ($8-$26) of any niche in the shortlist
- Evergreen — centuries of untold economic stories
- "Third Lane" positioning avoids YMYL termination risk
- Your AI stack handles the visual production. The bottleneck is scripting, which is where YOUR editorial judgment adds irreplaceable value
- Strong beyond-ads monetization (fintech sponsors, book affiliate, digital products)
- Cross-validated by 3 independent source videos

The tradeoff you're accepting: slower production per video (deep research + scripting) in exchange for the highest RPM and strongest long-term defensibility.

### If Running TWO Channels:

**Primary: M4 Animated Economic Documentary** (high RPM, slower production)
**Secondary: E5 Science Explainers — Neuroscience** (faster production, solid RPM, educational sponsors)

Why this pair works:
- Different audiences — no cannibalization
- Different production speeds — economics for depth, neuroscience for volume
- Both are evergreen
- Both support educational sponsors (some sponsors fit both channels)
- Neuroscience can produce 3-4 videos/week while economics produces 1-2/week

### What I'd Warn You Against:

1. **Don't start with Stoicism** unless you've verified via Social Blade that Zen-style channels are still growing in July 2026. The copycat wave warning from V9 is the strongest negative signal in the entire dataset. First-mover advantage is gone.

2. **Don't start with Easy tier if your goal is high RPM.** Easy-tier niches will get you producing faster, but RPM ranges are lower and moats are weaker. If "high RPM AND high views" is non-negotiable, you need Medium or Hard tier.

3. **Don't skip the validation steps.** I've given you specific checks for each niche. The source video data is creator-reported and months old. Your vidIQ + Social Blade + Google Trends checks will tell you the CURRENT state. 30 minutes of validation could save you months of work in a dying niche.
