## Production Pipeline (Per Video — End to End)

### Step 1: TOPIC & ASSET VALIDATION (30 Mins)
→ **vidIQ:** Check trending crossover keywords (e.g., "Spiderman vs Skibidi Toilet").
→ **gta5-mods.com:** Download a free Mega Ramp or Obstacle `.xml` file.
→ **AddonPeds:** Inject the required superhero skins (free).
→ **JulioNIB:** Verify superpower scripts are active.

### Step 2: SCENE PLANNING (No Scripting) (10 Mins)
→ Write down the 3-5 minute sequence (e.g., "Hulk jumps off ramp in green car. Spiderman dances. Venom attacks.")
→ No `/prompt-forge` needed. This is pure visual dopamine planning.

### Step 3: STAGING & IN-GAME RECORDING (45 Mins)
→ Load the `.xml` map via **Menyoo Object Spooner**.
→ Spawn peds and lock them using **Scene Director** Numpad macros.
→ Use JulioNIB auto-combat scripts to automate fights.
→ Press F1 to record the chaos in a single, un-cut 3-5 minute take.

### Step 4: CINEMATICS & OBS CAPTURE (The Render Skip) (30 Mins)
→ Open Rockstar Editor. Detach camera (Free Cam).
→ Add low-angle shots, pans, and cinematic zooms to bypass the "Reused Content" flag.
→ **CRITICAL:** Hide the HUD (press H) and screen-record the playback using **OBS**. Do *not* export via Rockstar Editor (takes too long).

### Step 5: ASSEMBLY & SOUND DESIGN (30 Mins)
→ Drop OBS footage into **CapCut**.
→ **Mute all game audio** (prevents copyright/mature strikes).
→ Add royalty-free upbeat music (Kevin MacLeod) and spam cartoon SFX (boings, whooshes) to prove "human editorial effort" for monetization.
→ Append 15 minutes of your *previously uploaded* GTA videos to the end to hit the 20-minute mark.

### Step 6: POLISH & PUBLISH (20 Mins)
→ **Thumbnail:** Take a Rockstar Editor F12 screenshot. Use Photoshop. Crank vibrance to +100. Add white outer glow to the hero, red arrows, and scale the villain up 5x.
→ **vidIQ:** Optimize title (aim for 85+ score) and copy-paste character-counted tags.
→ Upload Long-form video. 
→ **The Bridge:** Cut the craziest 10 seconds into a Short, and use the YouTube Studio **"Related Video" link** to drive traffic to the long-form video.

## ADHD Rules (Retained & Enforced)
1. **Production before consumption.** No watching YouTube until your 3-5 minute OBS clip is recorded.
2. **One thing at a time.** Don't download 50 mods at once. Download the exact 3 mods needed for today's video.
3. **Phone in another room** during the 7-hour deep work block.
4. **If you've been "planning the set" for more than 30 minutes, you're procrastinating.** Load a pre-made XML map and press record.
