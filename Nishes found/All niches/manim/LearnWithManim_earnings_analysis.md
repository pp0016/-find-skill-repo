## The Honest Math — What They Actually Earned

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These vidIQ estimates are **generous upper bounds**, not reality. Here's why:

### Problem 1: The channel was almost certainly NOT monetized for the first 1-2 years

- Joined March 2020, first videos uploaded mid-2020
- 7,040 subs NOW, after 6 years. YouTube Partner Program requires 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours
- With only 10 videos and slow growth, they likely didn't hit 1,000 subs until 2022-2023 at earliest
- **All views earned before monetization = $0 in ad revenue**
- A huge chunk of these videos' lifetime views came in the first 1-2 years (when educational content gets discovered via search), which was before monetization

### Problem 2: The RPM is low and realistic

- NexLev reports channel RPM of **$1.80** — this is below the category average of $2.00
- For 3-4 minute educational videos, this is expected. Short educational videos get fewer mid-rolls (you need 8+ minutes for mid-roll ads)
- None of these videos are long enough for mid-roll ads, so revenue is purely from pre-roll ads

### Problem 3: Actual RPM with geographic mix

- 47.2% US, 17.6% India, 13.5% UK, 11.4% Canada, 10.3% Australia
- The **82.4% Tier 1 traffic** (US/UK/CA/AU) is excellent for RPM
- But Indian traffic (17.6%) pulls the effective RPM down
- The $1.80 RPM already reflects this mix

### My Realistic Estimate

| Scenario | Assumption | 3-Video Total |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| **vidIQ Mid Estimate** | All views monetized, US education RPM | **$3,679** |
| **Realistic (adjusted)** | ~60% of views monetized (late monetization), actual $1.80 RPM | **~$660 – $800** |
| **Most Likely** | Factoring in that most views came early + not all views serve ads | **~$500 – $700** |

### Calculation (Realistic):

```
Total views across 3 videos: 613,100
Estimated % of views that occurred after monetization: ~55-60%
Monetized views: ~337,000 – 368,000
Channel RPM: $1.80
Not all views serve ads (ad fill rate ~60-70% for this niche)
Effective monetized views: ~202,000 – 258,000

Revenue = (202,000 to 258,000) × $1.80 / 1,000
Revenue = ~$364 – $464
```

> [!CAUTION]
> **The real total from these 3 videos over 6 years is likely $400 – $700.**
> vidIQ's $3,679 mid-estimate assumes every single view was monetized from day one, which is almost certainly false for a channel that took years to reach 1,000 subs.
